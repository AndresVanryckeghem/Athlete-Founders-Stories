Playbook: how one small act of kindness became a global movement

CYCLING WITHOUT AGE

How a Copenhagen cycling advocate who noticed elderly people disappearing from the modern cityscape rang the doorbell of a local nursing home with a modified cargo bike, watched a woman who had stopped speaking talk nonstop for an hour, named the resulting organization after a blind passenger who said she could still smell the flowers, and built it into a global network of volunteer pilots proving that the bicycle's appeal has nothing to do with age.
