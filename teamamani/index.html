Playbook: how a coffee label named for peace became a cycling movement

TEAM AMANI

How a war crimes lawyer who spent twelve years interviewing survivors of atrocities inherited a charity project in Africa he despised, replaced it with a simple coffee label named for the Swahili word for peace, met a Kenyan cyclist named Sule Kangangi who redirected the entire mission, and after Kangangi's sudden death at a Vermont gravel race, found himself reluctantly leading a movement that reversed who travels to whom, built a home for forty athletes on land funded by a Meta advertising deal, and watched a women's team finish first, second, and third by ten minutes on the way to a three year goal of reaching the Tour de France Femmes.
