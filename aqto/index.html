Playbook: how pouring wine at a bike race led to a decade of cycling tours through Italy

A'QTO

How a daughter of Italian immigrants who was raised to speak English and burst into tears the moment she first landed in Italy at twenty met her husband while pouring wine at a bike race, turned his blog post about a Tuscan cycling event into a tours company after a stranger asked to join him, built a fourteen guest cap and a non riding partner program to protect what made it special, and after ten years of running it alongside a full consulting career, split her life between six months in Italy and six months in Australia while deliberately choosing to stay small on purpose.
